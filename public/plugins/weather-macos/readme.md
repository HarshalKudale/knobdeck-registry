# Weather

Weather on your keys and wheels, for any place, on Windows and macOS. Data comes from
[Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com): free, no account and no API key.

## What it does

- Weather: temperature and conditions with a matching icon, refreshed every 10 minutes. Press it
  to see today's high, low and "feels like".
- Forecast (7 days) wheel: high/low and chance of rain per day.
- Next 24 hours wheel: temperature every 3 hours.
- Air Quality: US AQI or European AQI with its band (Good, Moderate…).

Location is a city ("Pune", or "Portland, Oregon" to pick the right one) or coordinates
("18.52, 73.86"). Celsius or Fahrenheit per key.
